Vocabulary & Synonyms

Holidays: hotel, coast, plane, ticket, postcard, beach, city, sightseeing, holiday, journey.

School: teacher, classroom, lesson, uniform, rule, polite, responsible, homework, break.

Emotions: happy/glad, sad/unhappy, scared/frightened, angry/furious, tired/drowsy.

Synonyms: huge/enormous, noisy/rowdy, simple/easy, quickly/rapidly, close/near.

Antonyms: big/small, hot/cold, dry/wet, front/back, early/late, fast/slow, arrogant/humble.

Grammar Lab

Simple Past

Regular verbs: play → played; visit → visited; study → studied; stop → stopped.

Irregular verbs: go → went, see → saw, eat → ate, take → took, find → found, lose → lost, get → got, meet → met, spend → spent, make → made, have → had, be → was/were.

Positive: I visited London. Negative: I didn't visit London. Question: Did you visit London?

After did/didn't always use the base form: Did you go? Not "Did you went?"

Connectives

because = weil · so = deshalb · but = aber · when = als/wenn · after = nachdem/nach · first/then/in the end = zuerst/dann/am Ende.

-ed pronunciation

/t/: looked, watched, cooked · /d/: played, cleaned, enjoyed · /ɪd/: visited, waited, tasted.
